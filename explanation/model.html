Entities, ops, snapshots ​

The whole substrate is three primitives. Get these and the rest is plumbing.

Entity ​

{id, kind, node, indexDoc, health}

  • kind — free-form tag; the integration's namespace (sepia-session, campaign, …).
  • indexDoc — opaque JSON for list views. Hypha merges and columnizes it; meaning belongs to the integration.
  • health — liveness/lock state reported by the owner node.

Op ​

{entity, kind, payload} — submitted to an entity's owner node.

  • kind is namespaced <integration>.<verb> (sepia.prompt, campaign.start); entity.* is the reserved substrate namespace (entity.delete only, today).
  • payload is opaque to hypha — the owning plane interprets it.
  • Ops are idempotent-keyed and TTL'd in the outbox; Turn-class ops dead-letter on first failure.

Snapshot ​

{entity, ref, bytes} — the migration primitive.

Serialize on the owner (GET /api/entities/{id}/snapshot), import on the target (POST /api/entities/snapshot). Bytes are opaque end-to-end; resume semantics are the integration's (campaign-snapshot/v1, sepia session IR export, …).

Why generic ​

The distributed abilities — sync, arbitration, outbox, transfer, pairing — are orchestration-domain but workload-agnostic. Shaping them around sessions was the mistake ADR-0091 corrected; shaping them around entities is what lets bupha jobs, sturn claims, or cicada schedules become kinds without touching this repo.
